* **_More of The Fancy Restaurants In Dallas_**

Dallas offers plenty of options for travelers looking for exciting[ vacations to enjoy with their family](https://www.thetravel.com/best-affordable-family-friendly-vacations/) or have a fun night out in couple on the town. [The Design District](https://dallasdesigndistrict.com/), [Six Flags Over Dallas](https://www.sixflags.com/overtexas), [Perot Museum of Nature and Science](https://www.perotmuseum.org/), [Klyde Warren Park](https://www.klydewarrenpark.org/), [Reunion Tower](https://reuniontower.com/), and [Trinity Groves](https://www.trinitygroves.com/) are some of the town’s top attractions. [The magnificent city is a blend of culture, fun, and history](https://www.thetravel.com/things-to-do-in-dallas/), but trips are never complete without trying exceptional food in unique restaurants where all cravings and fantasies are satisfied. Culinary happiness is guaranteed in Dallas. The area is recognized for its local specialties like BBQ and beef, which date back to a period when [cattle reigned and cowboys ruled the region](https://www.thetravel.com/is-texas-bandera-the-cowboy-capital-of-the-world/). Therefore, here are 14 fancy Dallas restaurants worth splurging on.

**_More of The Fancy Restaurants In Dallas_**

Dallas is full of excellent dining options and has something for every dining experience. ## 14 The French Room 

The French Room is among the top restaurants in the United States, having several important honors to its credit. The food is inspired by French gastronomy traditions and methods and is served as a five-course menu with the [possibility of wine pairings](https://www.thetravel.com/what-foods-dont-go-with-wine/). The French Room exudes the glitz and timeless elegance of European creativity, including both art and cuisine, and will keep guests enthralled throughout their meal.

* **Location:** 1321 Commerce St, Dallas, TX 75202, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** Closed on Monday and Tuesday. The restaurant serves guests from 11:00 AM to 2:30 PM Wednesday through Sunday.

## 13 Perry's Steakhouse And Grille 

This fancy restaurant started with a [modest butchery for Perry’s family](https://cdn.websites.hibu.com/ead5834f3e0c4c9a91e57ea1e10c9e1d/files/uploaded/joyride-advisors-kings-perrys-restaurants-brand-book-spring-2019.pdf). Today, Perry's Steakhouse and Grille is one of the country's most acclaimed steakhouses. It expanded and has branches all over the United States. The eatery has stayed loyal to its local butcher shop beginnings with top-notch variety and service.

* **Location:** 2100 Olive St, Dallas, TX 75201, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** It opens daily. The eatery serves guests from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM but closes at 9:00 PM on Sunday. On Friday, it receives diners from 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM.

## 12 Mansion Restaurant 

This exquisite restaurant in Dallas is elegant and magnificent, with gorgeous architecture and design sprinkled throughout the space. It is like walking into a castle, with colored windows, a wonderful fireplace, intricate finishing, and majestic archways. The food, which is a blend of [American cuisine](https://www.thespruceeats.com/defining-american-cuisine-102172) with French elements and is matched with a wide selection of wines, is equally impressive. The Mansion Restaurant is unquestionably one of the city's most opulent eateries.

* **Location:** 2821 Turtle Creek Blvd, Dallas, TX 75219, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** The restaurant opens daily.  
  * For breakfast: 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM;
  * For lunch: 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM;
  * For dinner:  
    * 6:00 PM to 9:30 PM on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday
    * 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM on Tuesday and Wednesday
    * It doesn’t serve dinner on Sunday and Monday.

## 11 Town Hearth 

Town Hearth has a strange, but marvelous interior, designed by the restaurant’s chef, Nick Badovinus, with over 60 chandeliers, an MG Roadster placed in front of the kitchen, and a 1974 Ducati Sport parking next to the bar. Delicious food accompanies this lovely atmosphere. The eatery is well known for its supreme steaks.

* **Location:** 1617 Market Center Blvd, Dallas, TX 75207-3915, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** It opens daily at 5:00 PM and closes at 9:00 PM on Sunday and Monday; at 10:00 PM on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday; and at 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday.

## 10 Fearing's Restaurant 

Fearing’s Restaurant is situated in [The Ritz-Carlton Dallas](https://www.ritzcarlton.com/en/hotels/dallas). With wide windows that look out onto the hotel's garden, leather sofas, and timber decors all around, guests get the impression that they are embraced by modern southern hospitality. Chef Dean Fearing, known as the "[Father of Southwestern Cuisine](https://honestcooking.com/chef-dean-fearing-interview-ritz-carlton/)", produces an upgraded American cuisine with a distinct southwestern flavor.

* **Location:** 2121 McKinney Avenue, Dallas, TX 75201, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** Closed on Sunday and Monday. It welcomes visitors from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM except for Tuesday, and every day from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM.

## 9 The Capital Grille 

The Capital Grille visitors have to get prepared to spend an exceptional evening enjoying the real meaning of fine dining meals. The elegant restaurant provides fresh seafood, dry-aged steaks, and a huge international variety of wines in a wonderful atmosphere.

* **Location:** 500 Crescent Ct, Dallas, TX 75201, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** The restaurant opens Monday through Thursday from 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM; Friday from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Saturday from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM; and Sunday from 10:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

## 8 SER Steak + Spirits 

Steakhouses are often identified as casual restaurants, but SER Steak + Spirits will take its diners to a fancier level than just eating a simple steak. This elegant restaurant is known for its excellent cuts of meat and a wonderful seafood menu. As guests take in panoramic scenes of Dallas, they will try some of the juiciest, tastiest steaks from the South.

* **Location:** 2201 N Stemmons Fwy, Dallas, TX 75207, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** Closed on Sunday. It welcomes visitors Monday through Thursday from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM and opens till 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday.

## 7 Bullion 

Bullion on South Record Street is one of the greatest places to try excellent fancy food. The sophistication of French food is served in a relaxed ambiance at this upscale dining establishment. Celebrating the event in Bullion is an awesome idea, whether visiting for a romantic night with the beloved one or a small birthday dinner gathering with friends. Moreover, the restaurant offers a fantastic view of Dallas!

* **Location:** 2121 McKinney Avenue, Dallas, TX 75201, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** It opens from Tuesday to Saturday. The eatery welcomes visitors from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM except for Tuesday, and every day from 5:30 PM to 10:00 PM.

## 6 Carte Blanche 

In June 2021, Carte Blanche opened in Dallas. For breakfast, it offers a variety of house-baked goodies created with the finest butter and local ingredients. As for dinner, the restaurant has mouth-watering 5 and 12-course set menus that diners can choose from. [Awarded with 5 Forbes stars, Carte Blanche](https://dallas.culturemap.com/news/restaurants-bars/04-26-22-forbes-travel-guide-carte-blanche-fearings/) is the first restaurant in Texas to get this rating, and it is also the United States’ unique five-star eatery.

* **Location:** 2114 Greenville Ave Dallas, TX 75206, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** Closed on Monday. The restaurant serves guests from 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM Thursday through Sunday. For afternoon guests, the restaurant opens from 5:00P M to 9:00 PM Tuesday through Saturday.

## 5 Monarch 

[Danny Grant, a two-Michelin-star chef](https://www.chicagogourmet.org/page/DannyGrant/Danny-Grant-.htm#:~:text=Chef%20Grant%20was%20the%20youngest,Chicago%20Tribune%20and%20Chicago%20magazine.), and his colleagues created Monarch, a modern Italian restaurant. The ultimate embodiment of fine dining in Dallas is waiting for visitors. This stylishly furnished environment is combined with exquisite fresh and light food. Pasta is handcrafted, steaks are sublimely cooked, and seafood is freshly picked.

* **Location:** 1401 Elm St Fl 49 Dallas, TX 75202, United States
* **Restaurant days and hours:** The restaurant opens daily at 5:00 PM and closes at 11:00 PM on Friday and Saturday and at 10:00 PM on the other days.

## 10 Aubergine 

Aubergine is one of the top restaurants in Canberra. It was located at 18 Barker Street Griffith Canberra ACT. For the last nine consecutive years, Aubergine has continuously earned favorable coverage and ratings in the mainstream, earning two SMH Chef Hats. Their cuisines are sophisticated and new but the traditional basics of their concepts are still there. They offer a total of five main course menus that cost 130 dollars for each person. The restaurant was supported and supplied by local farmers, therefore their ingredients are all fresh and organic. The business operates for dinner from Wednesday through Saturday. Early bookings and reservations form online are accepted.

## 9 Corella Restaurant And Bar 

Corella Restaurant and bar was [shaped by modern Australian cuisines](https://www.thetravel.com/weird-foods-from-australia/). It was one of the 10 best new restaurants in Australia that have been featured by Gourmet Traveller magazine. It was an excellent wine bar delivering sophisticated modern Australian meals made by using local ingredients. In fact, every item on their menu incorporates at least one native ingredient. Also, it’s the ideal spot for a casual drink, a gathering with friends, or a romantic dinner, with a good wine list and handcrafted cocktails. Moreover, Corella is located in the city of Lonsdale Street in Braddon and is open from Wednesday to Sunday, midday until late evening.

## 8 RAKU 

RAKU is a Japanese restaurant located at 138 Bunda Street in Canberra. It is a vegetarian-friendly, vegan, and gluten-free restaurant. RAKU blends Japanese cuisine history's delicacy and artistry with the lively energy of current Australian meals. They offer the most nutritious and freshest ingredients for their customers. Sashimis are also served on an artistic plate, for the guests’ visual delight as they enjoy their food. In RAKU, if you still haven’t decided what to order, they have three sets of menus to offer. A menu that includes an expedited lunch option and includes six meals for 40 dollars per person. Moreover, the business starts to operate at 11:30 am and closes at 10 pm from Monday to Sunday.

## 7 XO 

XO is known for its unique, modern take on classic southeast Asian cuisine based on a dedication to straightforward, authentic food and high quality of service. With meals like Asian spaghetti bolognese, shantung bao sliders, and a dish of Vietnamese duck leg prepared with a twist, the cuisines of XO are trendy. On the open wall, different types of wine are stocked and serve as Canberra vintages. XO restaurant is suitable for groups of up to 10 people and is vegetarian friendly.

## 6 Pilot 

The Pilot has been awarded as the Restaurant of the Year in Canberra. They are known for a restaurant that has an a la carte menu which is updated every two days, and thus a set of cuisines menu. Their steaks, broth, and cooked pies have an excellent sense of taste. Also, its cocktail menu is varied and can meet the demands of all tourists. The chefs are working hard to deliver a great variety of food that came from other countries’ delicacies. The business operates from Wednesday to Sunday.

## 5 Rebel Rebel 

Elegantly designed premises inspired by David Bowie, and it is well-known for its wonderful cuisine and reasonable costs. Their mission is to give their customers what they want. Its wine comes from both domestic and foreign grapes, and they also offer by-the-bottle champagne that includes selections from all of the major growing areas. With global influence in the form of various ingredients, their cuisines are contemporary. Furthermore, the restaurant is located at 23 Marcus Clarke Street, Acton and it is open from Monday to Friday, noon until 10 pm, and Saturday, 8 am until 10 pm.

## 4 Lilotang

[Texas is the most walkable US state](https://www.thetravel.com/most-walkable-us-state-texas/), and Fort Worth, often called "Cowtown," is a great example of why. This place will forever be in my heart; whenever friends come to town and ask me where they should go, I always say the Fort Worth Stockyards. E. Exchange St. is lined with western wear shops, upscale boutiques, bars, and restaurants. Be sure to cross Main Street to W. Exchange St. to shop at the General Store for Texas souvenirs and the famous boot store, M.L. Leddy. Mule Alley is the latest trend in the Stockyards. Don't miss it.

Parking is no longer free. Plan on spending $15 on parking for the day.

## 5 Panther Island

Kayaking on the Trinity River in Fort Worth

 If you're visiting downtown Fort Worth and want some sunshine, I recommend Panther Island. It's a 3-to-4-minute drive from the downtown Tarrant County Courthouse. It's one of the best outdoor areas in Dallas-Forth Worth for events, and you'll get the beautiful downtown Fort Worth skyline as a backdrop.

The beautiful 120-acre [Fort Worth Botanic Garden](https://fwbg.org/) is Texas' oldest public botanic garden. It comprises over twenty specialty gardens, a forest boardwalk, a water garden, a tropical paradise garden, and several gardens highlighting plants native to north central Texas. Kids will love exploring the Japanese Garden, the Rose Garden, and the kid-friendly nature center.

Springtime is our favorite time to visit because it's not hot, and the spring wildflowers are abundant. It's open every day of the week from 8 am to 6 pm.

Parking is free except when there is an event.

## 1 Fort Worth Cultural District 

### Award-winning world-c_lass museums, performance art, and galleries_ 

 

View of the Fort Worth, Texas skyline

Credit: Shutterstock

Visiting the Fort Worth Cultural District would be the ultimate Texas trip for history buffs who want to see the museums of contemporary art and Texas history in Fort Worth.

This area is home to five museums: The [Kimbell Art Museum](https://kimbellart.org/) (home to the only Michelangelo in the Western hemisphere), the [Modern Art Museum of Fort Worth](https://www.themodern.org/) (best design building in Texas), the [Fort Worth Museum of Science and History](https://www.fwmuseum.org/) (fun the entire family - it's a kid's museum at heart), the [Amon Carter Museum of American Art](https://www.cartermuseum.org/) (explores the diversity and complexity of American creativity over the last 200 years), and the [National Cowgirl Museum And Hall of Fame ](https://www.cowgirl.net/)(celebrates the historical women of the American West).

Along with the above, Dickies Arena, Will Rogers Coliseum, the Fort Worth Omni Theater, and the Fort Worth Botanic Gardens are located in this area. Plus, there are a dozen restaurants and shops along the way. I don't think anyone will get bored in this district of Fort Worth.
